What we work on

Most projects combine two or three of these.

  • Custom software & automation

    When a process depends on manual review, or on a spreadsheet only one person can maintain, we build an application and database to do the work.

    What's included
    • Applications and databases built around your process
    • Automated data validation, anomaly detection and correction
    • Integration with the systems you already use

  • Data, KPIs & monitoring

    We connect your sales, operations and accounting data and build dashboards that refresh on their own.

    What's included
    • Choosing and defining KPIs with management
    • Connecting your ERP, CRM and other systems
    • Automated dashboards and reports
    • Performance metrics for teams and individuals

  • Pricing & profitability

    We work out which products, customers and contracts make money, and build pricing models that are tested on your past results before you change a price.

    What's included
    • Profitability by product, customer, contract or channel
    • New pricing models, or a review of the one you have
    • Estimating how a price change affects volume and margin

  • Forecasting & reserving

    Forecasts of sales, costs and cash with a best and a worst case, and the assumptions written down. If you carry future obligations such as warranties, returns or claims, we estimate how much to set aside.

    What's included
    • Sales, cost and cash flow forecasts
    • Scenario and sensitivity analysis
    • Reserves and provisions
    • Budget models your team can update

  • Cloud costs (FinOps)

    If your cloud bill grows faster than your usage, we find where the money goes and set up monthly cost reporting by team, product or client.

    What's included
    • Audit of cloud usage and spend
    • Cost allocation and tagging
    • Right-sizing recommendations
    • Budget alerts

Also available: part-time financial oversight for companies without a full-time finance lead, including month-end review, budgets and reporting to owners and lenders.

How a project runs

Four steps, with something concrete to show at the end of each one.

  1. Diagnose

    We review your data and current reports with you and agree on the question the project has to answer.

    You receiveA written scope and estimate

  2. Model

    We build the analysis and test it on your past data. Every assumption is documented.

    You receiveThe model and its test results

  3. Implement

    We turn the model into an application, a dashboard or an automated report connected to your systems.

    You receiveA working tool, documentation and training

  4. Monitor

    We compare results with actuals and adjust the model when your business changes.

    You receivePeriodic reviews and updates
